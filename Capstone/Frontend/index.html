About Placemento

Placemento is an advanced platform dedicated to bridging the divide between academic learning and industry requirements. Our mission is to empower students by providing them with a clear, analytical view of their current skill sets compared to market demands.

Through our comprehensive Skill Gap Analysis engine, students can visualize their proficiency, discover personalized learning paths, and ultimately connect with premier placement opportunities tailored to their unique profile.
